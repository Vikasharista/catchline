# Nordcap award rules (buyer policy)

- Compare on EUR/kg **net** weight, **DAP Boulogne**.
- Eligible only with a BRCGS or IFS certificate valid on the award date (2026-09-30).
- Shrimp/squid glaze above 10% must be declared; price converted to net weight.
- Phosphates (E450-E452) not accepted on raw shrimp for the retail range.
- Farmed lines need antibiotic residue testing on every lot.
- Wild lines need an EU IUU catch certificate.
- Max 60% of any species' volume with one supplier (dual sourcing).
- Conditional discounts are shown separately and not used for ranking unless the buyer accepts the condition.
